Joao Monteiro

Assistant Professor · EIEF, Rome

I am an Assistant Professor at the Einaudi Institute for Economics and Finance (EIEF) in Rome. My research covers corporate finance, international economics, macroeconomics, and the economics of conflict.

I received my Ph.D. in Finance from Northwestern University (Kellogg) in 2023. I co-organize the Rome Junior Finance Conference with Luana Zaccaria and Spyridon Lagaras.
